Experiment - Self report / Reflections

When I watch the picture while listening to music, I feel as if the circle-triangle-square knows when to move, and it does it with a joyful character.

It is like there are small little stories in the fabric between the notes, the movement, the colors. Or perhaps it is sort of like additional music. The rhythm is the common language between visuals and music, they are polyphonic voices, contributing to a Gestalt, made from more than either the visuals or the music alone. It's made from their relationship, their dance.

At times, the picture seems to play with us. As if to feign a move. Then perhaps reluctantly, then perhaps all in, then again dipping or then 'deliberately' out of sync.

It is the fact that the relationship is weaved by the rhythm of the movements, together with this 'character' that I perceive the picture to have, it's non-zero 'expressiveness', that licenses me to describe this as a 'dance'.

Character Detection

If there is a dance, there is a dancer. A non-zero character having entity.

During the Heider-Simmel Illusion, the user perceives the shapes to be characters that follow a kind of storyline. 'Big triangle is angry', 'small triange is scared', and so on.

Our perception of character, and 'personhood' is not tied to sensor data corresponding to humans nor animals. We readily attribute mind to shapes moving on a screen. It is as if we have another pair of eyes in our minds, that see not the visual scene but that see animal-like or person-like, goal-having, feeling-having, relationship-having 'agents'.

Hyperactive Agent Detection

Dawkins mused in one of his books:

From all animals that perceived rustling of savanna grass, the ones that erred on the side of caution and perceived a tiger were better off.

A false negative (real: wind, perceived: tiger) makes you anxious and jumping around a little to much. A false positive (real: tiger, perceived: wind) makes you tiger dinner, statistically speaking.

This is empirical. A bias of animal and human cognition towards agentness exists: Hyperactive Agent Detection.

(Incidentally this is a beautiful example of theoretical evolutionary biology contributing to the explanation of an aspect of the psyche).

Interpretation

My interpretation is that we can attribute music to static noise and see faces in random clouds (Pareidolia).

So too, we can perceive 'agentness' in random movement. 👈

(This is the main outcome of this blog post).

Just like our 'internal agent eyes' see characters in the Heider-Simmel Illusion, and just like our 'sensor' eyes see faces in clouds. Our 'internal agent eyes' can also see 'agents' in random noice.

That we have some kind of 'agent-perceiving' machinery lies at hand.

Just like the machinery that detects faces, even in clouds, the machinery that detects agents is 'on', and perhaps it is 'eager' to contribute to the story-line we use to make sense to the world.

There is also the concept of auditory pareidolia. I forget the details, but many people reported they hear 'a Christmas carol' when prompted to listen for it in static noise. Very eerie isn't it? There is something ghostly and perhaps insubstantial to it. Not to mention the association with the voices of the psychotic. It is the price we pay for the kind of perception we are endowed with. That weaves the fabric of the world by stories and the ideas that are not there. We see ideas, not sensor data. That much is certain.
