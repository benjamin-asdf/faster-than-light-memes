[[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._C._R._Licklider][J. C. R. Licklider]] is a super interesting person in the history of computers and cybernetics.

/Getting into the position to think/

he talks about how he needs to plot and manually calculate things before he can think.



My job as a tool developer is to provide you with the power to get into the position to think.

Reduce the friction of what is between your thinking and the model of the computer.

In other words, produce a layer of communication between the computer and the human.
